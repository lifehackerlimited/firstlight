Questions

Will I be charged during the trial?

No. You're charged on day 31 unless you cancel first. We'll email you before the trial ends, and you can cancel from your account page in two clicks.

Is the website itself free?

Yes, always. Paid plans pay for the extras: personal email briefings, AI summaries and the team API. They also keep the site ad-free.

Who handles my payment?

Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch Nascent. For paid plans Stripe acts as the reseller (merchant of record), so your receipt comes from Stripe and VAT is handled correctly wherever you live. Prices include VAT where it applies.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're charged and change your mind within 14 days, email us for a full refund. See the terms.
