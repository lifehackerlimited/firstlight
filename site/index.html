      About Nascent

      Nascent gathers the latest emerging-technology research, news, analysis, open-source projects, launches and learning material from public sources: preprint servers and journals, university and lab newsrooms, specialist outlets, Substack newsletters, Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt, GitHub, Hugging Face and educational channels. It is built on MCT, the Media Collection Tool.
