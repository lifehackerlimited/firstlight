Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Nascent is run by Betttech Global Ventures Ltd. · Company number 11753604 (England & Wales) · Registered office: 7 Clim Down, CT14 8EX, United Kingdom · info@betttech.co.uk. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. We collect as little as possible.

Reading the site

You don't need an account to read Nascent. The site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or tracking scripts. Saved stories, followed topics, muted sources, your theme and your For you preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on your device only. They're never sent to us, and you can clear them at any time from the account page.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Subscriptions and email digests

If you sign up for a plan (including the free weekly digest), we process your name, email address, plan, digest keywords and billing history to provide the service. The lawful basis is performance of a contract. For the free digest, the basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.

  • Stripe handles sign-up, payments, invoices, tax and the account portal, and is the reseller (merchant of record) for paid plans. Card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Resend delivers digest emails on our behalf.
  • GitHub runs the automated jobs that build the site and send digests.

These providers may process data in the United States under the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the UK–US Data Bridge or equivalent safeguards. We keep subscription and billing records for up to 6 years after your subscription ends to meet UK tax law, then delete them.

AI summaries

Some story summaries are written with an AI model (Anthropic's Claude). Only public article titles and excerpts are sent. No personal data is sent.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@betttech.co.uk. We reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
