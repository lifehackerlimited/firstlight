# Nascent outreach emails

Founding institution pilot: 3 months of Team free (30-day trial + code `PILOT` for 2 more months), first 25 organisations.
Pricing page section: https://nascent.today/pricing/#institutions

Tips
- Send from your own name, not a no-reply address. Short emails get replies; these are under 150 words.
- Personalise the first line every time (a centre they run, a recent paper, a news item). Fill in everything in [brackets].
- Tag links per audience so Google Analytics shows what works: `?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=uni` (or `rd`, `vc`).
- One follow-up after 5–7 working days. Then stop.
- UK note: emailing someone at their work address about something relevant to their role is fine under PECR, but always offer an easy opt-out and don't buy lists.

## When someone says yes

1. Send them https://nascent.today/pricing/#institutions and tell them to use code **PILOT** at Team checkout. They add keywords and up to 4 colleagues' emails there.
2. Their private data feed is created on the next refresh (within 3 hours), and its address is included automatically in every Team digest email.
3. Book the feedback call for around week 6, so there's time to convert before the free period ends (after about 3 months).
4. If a whole department or company wants it, offer an annual licence (FAQ on the pricing page). Suggested range: £500–£1,500/year, unlimited seats, invoice billing.
